Questions

Straight answers.

How much does junk removal cost?

Every job is a flat rate based on what you've got and how much of it — which is why the photo quote works so well. Text a picture and you'll get a real number, not a range that doubles when we show up. Single items start affordable; full cleanouts are priced by volume and labor.

Can you really come the same day?

Most days, yes. We run Tampa Bay daily and keep room in the schedule for same-day calls. The earlier you text, the better the odds.

What won't you take?

We can't haul hazardous materials — wet paint, chemicals, asbestos, fuel. Pretty much everything else — furniture, appliances, hot tubs, electronics, yard debris, construction scrap — we take.

Do I need to move anything before you arrive?

Nope. Point at it and we handle the rest — stairs, tight corners, heavy stuff included. We bring the muscle and the equipment.

Do you work with realtors and property managers?

All the time — pre-listing cleanouts, estate clear-outs, punch-list work, and quick turnarounds between tenants. Realtors are some of our best repeat clients. Ask about recurring service.

What happens to my stuff?

Whatever's still got life in it gets donated or rehomed; the rest is disposed of properly. We'd rather see a good couch get a second home than a landfill.
